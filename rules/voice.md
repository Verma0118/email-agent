# Voice

How the emails should sound. Rules in `writing-rules.md` and `email-types.md` win over anything here.
If `personal/style-corpus.md` exists, read its real anchor emails too; they show that user's own voice.

## Sound like a real student
- Plain, direct sentences. Some short, some longer. Not every sentence the same shape.
- Curious, not impressive. The email asks to learn from them; it does not pitch the sender.
- Specific beats polished. One true detail about them is worth more than three adjectives.

## Hooks
Bad (could go to anyone, or no human talks like this):
- "I noticed that you worked full time at [company] as a [role]."
- "Your work in AI caught my eye as a signal of where the industry is heading."
- "I was fascinated by your extensive background spanning multiple domains."

Good (specific, said out loud):
- "I saw you moved from a propulsion team at Boeing to certification work at Joby and wanted to ask about that switch."
- "I saw you wrote your thesis on composite wing joints and now work on airframe structures, and wanted to ask how much of that research carries over day to day."

## Company line
Wrong: "[Company] is an incredible company doing amazing work and I would love to be part of it."
Right: one sentence naming a real product, decision, or initiative, and what about it interests the sender.
Write it fresh for each company.

## Example: coffee_chat (cold), 150 words, fictional sender and recipient

```
Subject: STATE Student | Coffee Chat Request | Northwind Aero | Alex Rivera

Good Morning Dana!

I hope your week has been off to a good start! My name is Alex Rivera and I am a Junior at State University majoring in Mechanical Engineering. I am a Powertrain Lead on Formula Racing, a student team building a car each year for the FSAE competition.

I saw you moved from motorsport into battery pack design at Northwind and wanted to ask how that experience carried over. What I like about Northwind is that the new trainer aircraft is being built around a swappable pack, which is close to the cooling problems our team works on every season.

If you are available for a quick 20-30 minute chat sometime over the next two weeks, I would really appreciate it. Happy to work around your schedule and send a Calendar Invite.

I have attached my resume, and my portfolio is at alexrivera.dev. I look forward to hearing from you!

Best,
Alex Rivera
B.S. Mechanical Engineering
www.linkedin.com/in/alexrivera
alex@example.com
```

## Example: follow_up, 58 words

```
Hi Dana,

I wanted to follow up on my email from last week in case it got buried. I also saw Northwind just opened its summer internship applications, so I would be glad to hear how students usually end up on the battery team.

If you have 20 minutes sometime this week or next, I would really appreciate it!

Best,
Alex
```
Note: "I wanted to follow up" is fine; "I wanted to reach out" is banned.

## Example: reply

```
Hi Dana!

Thursday at 2:00 ET works great for me! I have sent a calendar invite for that time as well to lock it in.

Looking forward to it!

Best,
Alex
```
